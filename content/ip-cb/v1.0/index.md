Chef printable card aligned draft for Cronometer URL import. Ingredient grams are proposed conversions from the card; finished edible mass unknown until a batch is weighed. Leave unsalted until use. Flavor boosters (ginger, turmeric, star anise, cloves, cinnamon, Parmesan rind, dried shiitake) are optional experiments, not base ingredients.

**Tags:** `chef`, `instant-pot`, `batch`, `broth`, `sipping`

**Yield:** about 2.5 to 3 quarts finished

## Ingredients

- **1588 g** chicken bones and parts (roasted carcass, backs, necks, wings, or feet; ~3.5 lb midpoint of card 3 to 4 lb)
- **300 g** onion, large, quartered, skin on (~1 large)
- **122 g** carrots, cut into chunks (~2 medium)
- **80 g** celery ribs, cut into chunks (~2 ribs)
- **50 g** garlic, head halved crosswise (~1 head)
- **2** bay leaves
- **2 g** whole black peppercorns (~1 tsp)
- **few** parsley stems (optional)
- **15 g** apple cider vinegar (optional, ~1 tbsp; helps pull gelatin)
- **2600 g** cold water to Instant Pot max fill line (~11 cups; card says about 10 to 12 cups)

## Instructions

1. Optional: brown raw parts on Sauté in a little oil about 8 minutes. Skip for a roasted carcass.
2. Add everything and cover with cold water, no higher than the max fill line.
3. Pressure cook on High for 90 minutes, then natural release (about 30 minutes).
4. Strain through a fine-mesh sieve; discard solids.
5. Chill overnight and lift off the fat. It should set like loose jelly.
6. Weigh finished edible yield; record grams in CHANGELOG. Freeze in 1-cup and 4-cup portions. Fridge 4 to 5 days; freezer about 6 months.
