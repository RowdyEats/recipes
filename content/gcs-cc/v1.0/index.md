## Ingredients

- **1360 g** chicken (thighs or breasts), cut into 4 cm pieces (~3 lb)
- **680 g** frozen fire-roasted green chiles (~2 × 12 oz)
- **960 g** homemade chicken stock (~4 cups)
- **360 g** whole milk (1–2 cups, to taste; tames heat)
- **450 g** corn kernels (~3 cups)
- **12.5 g** kosher salt (2½ tsp Morton), plus more to taste
- **36 g** garlic, minced (~12 cloves; doubled spice set)
- **6 g** ground cumin (~1 tbsp)
- **2 g** Mexican oregano (~2 tsp)
- **4 g** black pepper (~2 tsp)

## Instructions

1. Brown chicken on Instant Pot Sauté (skip if using pulled rotisserie meat — add at end).
2. Add stock, chiles, corn, and spices; pressure cook.
3. Stir in milk off pressure, to taste. Sour cream spoonful for guests who want it milder.
4. Weigh finished edible yield; record grams in CHANGELOG for per-100g nutrition.
