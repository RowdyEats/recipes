Instant Pot Chicken Broth (Mild Curry Sipping)

Before you cook

  • Nutrition numbers are still cooking — status is “estimated pending weighed batch”, so we are not publishing a nutrition panel on this page yet. Ingredient grams below are for shopping and prep, not a finished weighed batch.
  • Yield lists servings for now; finished edible mass unknown until a batch is weighed and the recipe is updated.
  • Ingredient weights are as written on the card (proposed or card-aligned unless a CHANGELOG entry says they were weighed).

v1.1 morning-cup batch: same Instant Pot base as v1.0 with fresh ginger, fresh turmeric, cinnamon, and black pepper cooked in for a mild golden curry sip. Turmeric is measured for the whole pot (~0.45 g ground-equivalent per 240 g cup — a bit above the earlier 0.3 g jar draft, still culinary). Dedicated sipping batch — do not use this curry-seasoned broth as stock in green chile stew (GCS-CC); keep a plain unsalted cook (v1.0 or a future plain version) for stew. Leave the pot unsalted while cooking; season finished broth to ~710 mg sodium per 240 g cup to match current morning bouillon. Ingredient grams proposed until a finished batch is weighed.

chef instant-pot batch broth sipping mild-curry morning

Yield: about 2.5 to 3 quarts finished

Ingredients

  • 1588 g chicken bones and parts (roasted carcass, backs, necks, wings, or feet; ~3.5 lb midpoint of card 3 to 4 lb)
  • 300 g onion, large, quartered, skin on (~1 large)
  • 122 g carrots, cut into chunks (~2 medium)
  • 80 g celery ribs, cut into chunks (~2 ribs)
  • 50 g garlic, head halved crosswise (~1 head)
  • 45 g fresh ginger, sliced (~3 inches)
  • 35 g fresh turmeric root, sliced (~2.5 to 3 inches; gloves optional — stains; if no fresh, substitute 5.5 g / about 2¼ tsp ground turmeric)
  • 1 cinnamon stick, small (~2 to 3 g)
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 2 g whole black peppercorns (~1 tsp)
  • few parsley stems (optional)
  • 15 g apple cider vinegar (optional, ~1 tbsp; helps pull gelatin)
  • 2600 g cold water to Instant Pot max fill line (~11 cups; about 10 to 12 cups)
  • salt after straining to sodium target (see step 6; not added before pressure cook)

Instructions

  1. Optional: brown raw parts on Sauté in a little oil about 8 minutes. Skip for a roasted carcass.
  2. Add bones, aromatics, ginger, turmeric, cinnamon stick, bay, peppercorns, parsley, vinegar, and cold water no higher than the max fill line.
  3. Pressure cook on High for 90 minutes, then natural release (about 30 minutes).
  4. Strain through a fine-mesh sieve; discard solids (including the cinnamon stick).
  5. Chill overnight and lift off the fat. It should set like loose jelly.
  6. Weigh finished edible yield in grams; record in CHANGELOG. Season to ~710 mg sodium per 240 g cup: about 1.8 g table salt per 240 g (or batch salt grams ≈ finished_g ÷ 240 × 1.8). Stir, taste, adjust once.
  7. Freeze in 1-cup (~240 g) and 4-cup portions for morning cups. Fridge 4 to 5 days; freezer about 6 months. Log with Coach as IP-CB v1.1 + grams of cup eaten.
  8. Backup mornings (no thawed broth): keep Better Than Bouillon roasted chicken as before — that is not this recipe.

Related: plain stock for stew → GCS-CC (use unsalted / non-curry broth, not this pot). Prior plain sipping base → IP-CB v1.0.
